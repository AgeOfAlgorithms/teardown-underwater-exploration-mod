name = Underwater Exploration
author = AgeOfAlgorithms and Claude
description = Underwater play on any map with water. Crouch in water to dive and swim where you look, with an air tank (the blowtorch replaces the sledgehammer while you dive). Caustic light, darker and bluer with depth, bubbles, schools of fish, seaweed and corals on the sea floor, and every loose object floats, hangs or sinks by its material. Nothing changes on dry land. Other mods and maps can change the air, the fish, the plants and more: see API.md in the mod's folder.
