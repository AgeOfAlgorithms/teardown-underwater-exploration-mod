name = Underwater Exploration
author = AgeOfAlgorithms and Claude
description = Underwater play on any map with water. Crouch in water to dive and swim where you look; your air lasts 1 minute. Diver gear in your tool wheel (an air tank and flippers: 3.5 minutes of air, faster swimming, slower on land) and a harpoon gun from the spawn menu; the game's guns don't work under water. Caustic light, deeper is darker, bubbles, schools of fish, seaweed and corals, and every loose object floats, hangs or sinks by its material. Nothing changes on dry land. Options (host): how many fish, how much kelp and how many corals. With the McPuppet Inventory System, the tank (back slot), harpoon and flippers (feet slot) become its items. Other mods and maps can change the air, the visibility and more: see API.md in the mod's folder.
