name = Underwater Exploration
author = AgeOfAlgorithms and Claude
description = Underwater play on any map with water. Crouch in water to dive and swim where you look; your air lasts 45 seconds. Diver gear (an air tank and flippers: longer air, faster swimming, slower on land) and a harpoon gun from the spawn menu; the game's guns don't work under water. Caustic light, deeper is darker, bubbles, schools of fish, seaweed and corals, and every loose object floats, hangs or sinks by its material. Nothing changes on dry land. Other mods and maps can change the air, the fish, the plants and more: see API.md in the mod's folder.
